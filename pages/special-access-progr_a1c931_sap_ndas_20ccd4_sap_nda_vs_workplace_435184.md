## Introduction

A Special Access Program (SAP) non-disclosure agreement is fundamentally different from the confidentiality agreement that an employee signs when joining a private company. A commercial confidentiality agreement primarily protects an employer's business interests, such as trade secrets, customer data, or intellectual property. A SAP non-disclosure agreement, by contrast, is part of the United States national security system. It is signed only after government approval for access to specially protected classified information and imposes legal duties that extend well beyond the employment relationship. Those duties continue even after a person changes jobs, retires, or leaves the programme entirely.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[whs.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

This distinction becomes especially important within Special Access Programs, including waived and [unacknowledged SAPs]({{ 'unacknowledged/' | relative_url }}), where the existence of the programme itself may be tightly controlled. In that setting, the agreement is not simply a promise to an employer—it is a formal acceptance of government-imposed security obligations backed by classification law, security procedures, and, in some circumstances, criminal sanctions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

## Commercial secrecy versus classified access

Although both types of agreement require discretion, they protect different interests and operate under different legal frameworks.

A workplace confidentiality agreement normally exists to prevent the misuse of commercially valuable information. Employers use these agreements to protect inventions, software source code, pricing strategies, customer lists, manufacturing methods, financial plans, or other proprietary information. The agreement is usually governed by contract law and employment law, with disputes commonly resolved through civil litigation.

A SAP non-disclosure agreement serves an entirely different purpose. It protects classified national security information that has already been determined to require safeguards exceeding those applied to ordinary classified material. Executive Order 13526 defines a Special Access Program as imposing access and safeguarding requirements beyond those normally required for information at the same classification level because of exceptional vulnerability or potential damage from disclosure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">SA001 guide</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...Executive Order 13526, “Classified National Security Information,” defines a SA...</span></span></span>

This means that access is based on several cumulative requirements rather than employment alone:

* An appropriate security clearance.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uh6E4Zclt8k" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: youtube.com">[youtube.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">youtube.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Security Clearance Nondisclosure Agreements</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What OPM&#x27;s Proposed NDA Rule Could Mean for You...</span></span></span>
* A demonstrated need to know.
* Formal programme approval.
* Completion of an indoctrination briefing.
* Execution of the [SAP Indoctrination]({{ 'sapia/' | relative_url }}) Agreement before access begins.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/jobaids/sap/SAP_Briefings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">SAP Briefings and Debriefings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SAP Briefings and DebriefingsMarch 28, 2022 — In Addition to the Brief. ## Government authorisation changes the legal relationship

Perhaps the biggest difference is who grants access and who owns the information being protected.

With a commercial NDA, the employer generally controls the protected information and decides who may receive it.

With a SAP NDA, access is granted on behalf of the United States Government. DD Form 2836 states that the signer accepts special obligations in exchange for being granted access to SAP information and acknowledges that "special confidence and trust" are being placed in them by the government. The agreement repeatedly requires written authorisation from the government department or agency responsible for the SAP before protected information may be disclosed outside authorised channels.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

The agreement also requires the individual to verify that another person has actually been approved for access before discussing SAP information. Unlike an ordinary workplace NDA, assumptions based on job [title]({{ 'statute/' | relative_url }}), seniority, or organisational position are insufficient. Authorisation must be programme-specific.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

This reflects a core principle of SAP security: access belongs to individuals who have been specifically approved, not simply to employees of a particular organisation.

## Legal consequences extend beyond contract disputes

Violating an ordinary workplace confidentiality agreement typically exposes an employee to civil consequences. These may include dismissal, financial damages, injunctions preventing further disclosure, or claims for breach of contract.

A SAP NDA incorporates those practical employment consequences but also reminds the signer that unauthorised disclosure may violate federal criminal law. DD Form 2836 specifically references provisions of Title 18 of the United States Code relating to the unlawful disclosure of certain categories of classified information and states that the agreement does not waive the government's right to prosecute statutory violations. It also states that the government may seek court orders preventing disclosure in addition to other legal remedies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

This does not mean every breach automatically becomes a criminal prosecution. Rather, the agreement recognises that classified information is protected by legal authorities that exist independently of the contract itself. The NDA records the individual's acknowledgement of those duties; it does not create the underlying classification laws.

## Why the promise follows the person

Many workplace confidentiality agreements continue after employment ends, particularly regarding trade secrets. However, those continuing obligations generally exist because the former employee still possesses commercially sensitive information.

SAP obligations are broader because they are tied to authorised access to classified information rather than to an employment contract. The SAP Indoctrination Agreement expressly states that the obligations continue after access ends. During [debriefing]({{ 'debriefing/' | relative_url }}), individuals reaffirm those continuing responsibilities before leaving the programme. CDSE training materials emphasise that protection duties continue for life unless information is officially declassified or otherwise authorised for release.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA002-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Program Security Annual RefresherRemember, by signing the SAPIA in both the indoctrination briefing and the debriefing...</span></span></span>

This continuing obligation has several practical consequences:

* Former programme members must not disclose protected information after changing employers.
* Retirement does not end the obligation.
* Moving into another government agency does not automatically permit discussion of the SAP.
* The information remains protected until the government formally changes its classification or release status.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

The duty therefore follows the individual rather than the workplace.

## Public writing and prior review

Another feature that distinguishes SAP agreements from ordinary employment NDAs is the treatment of public communications.

Many commercial confidentiality agreements prohibit disclosure but do not routinely require former employees to submit books, articles, speeches, or manuscripts for government review before publication.

DD Form 2836 contains an explicit pre-publication review obligation. Individuals agree to submit writings or other materials that contain, describe, or may reasonably derive from SAP information before any public disclosure. The responsible government department or agency is given an opportunity to determine whether classified SAP information is present before publication proceeds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2836.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDspecial access program indoctrination agreementApril 16, 2007 — I hereby acknowledge that I have received a security indoctrinatio...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2007</span></span></span>

## Why the distinction matters

Readers familiar with workplace NDAs sometimes assume that a SAP agreement is simply a stricter version of the same concept. In reality, the two agreements belong to different legal systems.

A commercial confidentiality agreement primarily protects private economic interests through contractual obligations between employer and employee. A SAP NDA forms part of the government's classified-information protection framework. It is inseparable from security clearance procedures, programme-specific authorisation, indoctrination, continuing reporting obligations, formal debriefing, and the wider body of national security law governing classified information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/jobaids/sap/SAP_Briefings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">SAP Briefings and Debriefings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SAP Briefings and DebriefingsMarch 28, 2022 — In Addition to the Brief. For Special Access Programs—including waived and unacknowledged SAPs—the agreement therefore functions less as an ordinary employment document and more as a personal acknowledgement that access to exceptionally sensitive classified information carries enduring legal responsibilities independent of any particular employer.
