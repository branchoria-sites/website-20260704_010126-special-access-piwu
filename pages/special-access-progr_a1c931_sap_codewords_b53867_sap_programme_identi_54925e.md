## Introduction

Special Access Program (SAP) documents often require security markings on every paragraph, table, figure and annex, not just in the document header. Repeating a full programme nickname dozens or hundreds of times would make documents harder to read, increase the chance of inconsistent markings and consume valuable space. To solve this problem, the US Department of Defense assigns each SAP a short programme identifier (PID), typically a two- or three-character designator, which can be used in [portion markings]({{ 'portion-markings/' | relative_url }}) while preserving an unambiguous link to the underlying programme. Official guidance makes clear that this is a formatting and handling aid, not a substitute for the programme's formal identity or security controls.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>

## How two- or three-character identifiers work

A programme identifier is a compact abbreviation assigned to a specific SAP for marking purposes. Instead of repeating a full nickname or codeword in every portion marking, classifiers use the PID after the abbreviation "SAR" (Special Access Required).

For example, a paragraph containing Top Secret information from a single SAP might be marked:

* **TS//SAR-ABC**

rather than spelling out the programme's full nickname every time. The mapping between the PID and the actual programme is controlled within the authorised security system, so cleared personnel know which programme the identifier represents without requiring the complete name to appear repeatedly. Training published by the Center for Development of Security Excellence (CDSE) uses fictitious examples such as "SAM", "SGR" and "FIC" to illustrate this practice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideFor SAP documents, portion markings that include SAP information must include “S...</span></span></span>

This approach is especially valuable because portion markings appear throughout a document:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* before individual paragraphs;
* alongside tables and figures;
* within annexes and appendices; and
* in extracted or quoted sections.

</div>

A short identifier keeps these repeated markings compact while still indicating exactly which SAP handling channel governs that information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why banners may spell out names while portions use abbreviations

DoD marking policy deliberately distinguishes between the document banner and the repeated portion markings.

The banner line at the top and bottom of a SAP document serves as the primary handling instruction. It normally includes the overall classification together with "SPECIAL ACCESS REQUIRED" (or "SAR") and the programme nickname or codeword specified by policy. The banner is intended to provide the complete handling context when someone first encounters the document.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>

Portion markings have a different purpose. They identify the classification and access requirements for individual pieces of information inside the document. Because these markings may appear many times on a single page, the guidance permits the use of the assigned PID instead of repeatedly writing the complete programme name. CDSE training explicitly instructs that SAP portion markings should include "SAR" followed by the PID.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideFor SAP documents, portion markings that include SAP information must include “S...</span></span></span>

This separation provides two practical benefits:

* **Consistency.** Every portion follows a standard, compact syntax that is easier to apply correctly.
* **Readability.** Technical reports, engineering drawings and briefing papers remain usable even when every paragraph requires its own security marking.

The abbreviated form therefore supports accurate document handling rather than increasing secrecy by obscurity.

## Why short identifiers improve safety

The chief purpose of programme identifiers is administrative reliability.

When every paragraph carries a concise, standardised marking, reviewers are less likely to omit required controls or introduce inconsistent formatting during editing. Compact identifiers also simplify automated document generation, quality assurance checks and derivative classification workflows, where software may validate repeated portion markings against approved templates. Official marking guidance is built around this standardisation rather than around hiding the programme name from authorised users.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>

The identifiers also help when [multiple SAPs]({{ 'multiple-saps/' | relative_url }}) apply to different parts of the same document. Rather than inserting lengthy programme names throughout the text, each affected portion can reference the appropriate PID using a consistent syntax. CDSE guidance specifies that where multiple programmes apply, the PIDs are listed alphabetically and separated by slashes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideFor SAP documents, portion markings that include SAP information must include “S...</span></span></span>

## What short IDs can and cannot replace

The existence of a PID does not change the underlying security requirements.

A short programme identifier **can**:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* identify the applicable SAP in portion markings;
* provide a compact reference throughout a classified document;
* distinguish between multiple SAPs within individual portions; and
* support consistent application of approved marking rules.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>

</div>

A short programme identifier **cannot**:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* replace the formal programme nickname or codeword where policy requires it in the document banner;
* substitute for classification levels or dissemination controls;
* grant access to a programme; or
* reduce any need-to-know or special-access requirements.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVn5m_lszRQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: youtube.com">[youtube.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">youtube.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Special Access Program &#124; Wikipedia audio article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alternate Compensatory Control Measures (ACCM)...</span></span></span>

</div>

In other words, the PID is a shorthand label within an established marking system. It does not alter who may see the information or how that information is protected. Those decisions remain governed by the programme's security policies, classification guidance and authorised access procedures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>

## The key implementation principle

Programme identifiers demonstrate an important design choice in SAP marking policy: make security markings as concise as possible without sacrificing precision. By assigning each programme a unique two- or three-character identifier for repeated portion markings, the system reduces clutter while preserving a direct, controlled connection between every classified passage and the correct special-access programme. The abbreviated form is therefore an implementation tool for accurate document handling—not a replacement for the programme's formal identity or its security protections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...September 9, 2020 — 10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2020</span></span></span>
