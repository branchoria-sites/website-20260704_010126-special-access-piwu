## Introduction

The quickest way to tell whether a classified document is protected by a Special Access Program (SAP), Sensitive Compartmented Information (SCI), or both is to read its banner line. Modern United States classification markings deliberately separate the **classification level** from the **control systems** and **dissemination controls** that apply to the information. That structure allows a single document to show, at a glance, whether it contains SCI, SAP information, additional handling restrictions such as **NOFORN** ("not releasable to foreign nationals"), or a combination of these controls. The distinction is not cosmetic: it reflects different legal and administrative access systems, helping authorised readers determine which approval process governs the information and how it must be handled.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dami.army.pentagon.mil/site/sso/docs/RG/icd-710.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dami.army.pentagon.mil">[pentagon.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dami.army.pentagon.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 17, 2009 — 11 Sept 2009 — The classification and control markings system enables information sharing and includes all markings...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 17, 2009</span></span></span>

## Classification First, Controls After

The standard banner line follows a consistent hierarchy. The first element is always the national security classification:

* **CONFIDENTIAL**
* **SECRET**
* **TOP SECRET**

Only after the classification appear the additional control markings, separated by double forward slashes (`//`). These controls are organised into recognised categories rather than being added in arbitrary order. The [Intelligence]({{ 'intelligence/' | relative_url }}) Community's marking standards require the hierarchy to be applied consistently so both people and automated systems interpret documents the same way.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/FOIA/Authorized%20Classification%20and%20Control%20Markings%20Register%20V1.2.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.gov">[dni.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 17, 2008 — Non-US or JOINT documents must always have the US classification left blank and the banner line start with a double forwa...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 17, 2008</span></span></span>

For example, a document might begin with:

* `TOP SECRET//SI//NOFORN`
* `TOP SECRET//HCS//TK//NOFORN`

Here, **TOP SECRET** identifies the classification level. **SI**, **HCS**, or **TK** identify SCI control systems. **NOFORN** is not another compartment; it is a dissemination control that limits who may receive the information. The ordering itself conveys which marking belongs to which category.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/FOIA/Authorized%20Classification%20and%20Control%20Markings%20Register%20V1.2.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.gov">[dni.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 17, 2008 — Non-US or JOINT documents must always have the US classification left blank and the banner line start with a double forwa...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 17, 2008</span></span></span>

## The Marking Line That Distinguishes SCI from SAP

SCI and [SAP markings]({{ 'markings/' | relative_url }}) occupy different positions within the control-marking structure because they represent different access regimes.

SCI uses formally recognised Intelligence Community control systems, such as communications intelligence or imagery intelligence [compartments]({{ 'compartments/' | relative_url }}), whose authorised abbreviations are maintained in the CAPCO (Controlled Access Program Coordination Office) Register. Every unique SCI control appearing within document portions must also appear in the overall banner line so readers immediately know which intelligence compartments govern access.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/jobaids/information/IC-Markings-System-Register.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[cdse.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IC Markings System Register</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CDSE(U) Intelligence Community Markings System Register and...31 Dec 2016 — ▫ May not be used with any other dissemination control marki...</span></span></span>

SAP information is marked differently. Rather than using an SCI control-system abbreviation, the banner carries **SPECIAL ACCESS REQUIRED** followed by the approved programme nickname or authorised abbreviation. [Portion markings]({{ 'portion-markings/' | relative_url }}) normally abbreviate this as **SAR** together with the programme identifier. The programme name—not the classification level—signals that access depends on membership in that specific SAP rather than solely on SCI eligibility.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/FOIA/Authorized%20Classification%20and%20Control%20Markings%20Register%20V1.2.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.gov">[Director of National Intelligence]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 17, 2008 — Non-US or JOINT documents must always have the US classification left blank and the banner line start with a double forwa...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 17, 2008</span></span></span>

This visual distinction allows trained holders to recognise immediately whether access approval is governed by an Intelligence Community compartment or by a specific special-access programme.

## Why One Document Can Carry Both Labels

The marking system is designed so multiple protection systems can coexist on the same document without creating ambiguity.

A document may legitimately contain:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--example" markdown="1">

* a classification level (for example, **TOP SECRET**),
* one or more SCI control systems,
* one or more SAP markings,
* dissemination controls,
* foreign government or other authorised control markings where applicable.

</div>

The CAPCO Register specifically allows SCI and SAP markings to be combined when appropriate, with each category occupying its designated place in the banner line rather than replacing the others. The resulting banner communicates that a reader may need both SCI eligibility and separate SAP authorisation before access is permitted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/jobaids/information/IC-Markings-System-Register.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[cdse.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IC Markings System Register</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CDSE(U) Intelligence Community Markings System Register and...31 Dec 2016 — ▫ May not be used with any other dissemination control marki...</span></span></span>

This is one reason the common shorthand "TS/SCI" should not be interpreted as encompassing every highly protected programme. A document marked for a SAP carries an additional programme-specific access requirement beyond any [SCI controls]({{ 'sci-controls/' | relative_url }}) that may also appear.

## What NOFORN and Other Limits Add

Dissemination controls answer a different question from SCI or SAP markings. They regulate **how information may be shared after authorised access has been granted**.

Examples include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **NOFORN** – prohibits release to foreign nationals.
* **ORCON** – originator controls further dissemination.
* **REL TO** – authorises release only to specifically identified countries or partners.

</div>

These controls appear after the classification and compartment markings because they do not establish separate access compartments. Instead, they restrict distribution or release of information that has already been classified and, where applicable, compartmented. Intelligence Community Directive 710 explicitly treats dissemination controls as separate from both classification levels and controlled-access programmes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-710.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.gov">[dni.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICD 710</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Director of National IntelligenceICD-710.pdf21 Jun 2013 — The CAPCO Register and Manual contains detailed guidance on the proper use of d...</span></span></span>

For example:

* `TOP SECRET//SI//NOFORN`

does **not** mean NOFORN is another SCI compartment. It means the document contains SI-controlled intelligence that additionally may not be released to foreign nationals.

## Portion Markings Reinforce the Separation

The distinction between SCI and SAP is repeated throughout the document, not just at the top of the page.

Each classified paragraph, table, image or other portion receives its own portion marking indicating:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* its classification level;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://robert.sesek.com/static/files/Intelligence-Community-Authorized-Classification-and-Control-Markings-Register-and-Manual-V6.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: robert.sesek.com">[robert.sesek.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">robert.sesek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ISOO Implementing Directive, and ICD 710.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">issues at the lowest possible level in accordance with I...</span></span></span>
* any applicable SCI controls;
* any applicable SAP controls;
* any required dissemination restrictions.

</div>

The overall banner line then "rolls up" the highest classification and every unique control appearing anywhere in the document. ## Why the Separation Matters

The banner line is more than an administrative convention. It encodes several distinct security decisions into a standard format:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Classification** indicates the potential damage from unauthorised disclosure.
* **SCI markings** identify formal Intelligence Community compartments protecting intelligence sources, methods or related information.
* **SAP markings** identify programme-specific special-access requirements established outside the standard SCI compartment structure.
* **Dissemination controls** define who may receive or further distribute the information after authorised access.

</div>

Because each category occupies a defined place in the marking string, authorised readers can determine immediately which access systems apply and which restrictions govern handling. The result is a single, standardised line that separates SAP controls from SCI compartments while allowing both to appear together whenever the underlying information requires multiple layers of protection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/FOIA/Authorized%20Classification%20and%20Control%20Markings%20Register%20V1.2.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.gov">[dni.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 17, 2008 — Non-US or JOINT documents must always have the US classification left blank and the banner line start with a double forwa...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 17, 2008</span></span></span>
