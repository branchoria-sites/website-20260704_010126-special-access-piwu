## Introduction

Documents that contain information from more than one Special Access Program (SAP) present a practical marking problem: the banner line must immediately warn authorised readers that multiple [compartments]({{ 'compartments/' | relative_url }}) are involved without becoming an unreadable list of programme names. Current Department of Defense (DoD) marking guidance solves this by using different banner conventions depending on how many SAPs are represented. Two programmes are identified individually in the banner, while documents containing information from three or more SAPs replace the list with the generic phrase **“MULTIPLE PROGRAMS”**. The detailed programme identifiers are then carried elsewhere in the document and its [accountability]({{ 'accountability/' | relative_url }}) records rather than crowded into the page header. This approach preserves security, improves readability and reduces the risk of accidental disclosure while still providing handlers with the information they need.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideJuly 1, 2025 — When creating a banner line marking for two programs, add a singl...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Two programmes versus three or more

DoD guidance distinguishes between documents containing information from exactly two SAPs and those containing information from three or more.

For a document containing material from **two SAPs**, both programmes remain visible in the banner line. The standard [construction]({{ 'construction/' | relative_url }}) repeats the SAP control marking for each programme, separated by a single slash. A notional training example provided by the Center for Development of Security Excellence (CDSE) is:

* `TOP SECRET//SAR-BLUE FROG/SAR-MUDDY PATH`

This tells an authorised reader immediately which two programmes govern access while keeping the banner compact and standardised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideJuly 1, 2025 — When creating a banner line marking for two programs, add a singl...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2025</span></span></span>

Once **three or more SAPs** are involved, policy changes. Rather than continuing to append programme names, the banner simply states:

* `TOP SECRET//SAR-MULTIPLE PROGRAMS`<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://quizlet.com/964556449/dodm-520507-vol-4-flash-cards/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: quizlet.com">[quizlet.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">quizlet.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">dodm 520507 vol 4 flash cards</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoDM 5205.07 Vol 4 FlashcardsHow do you mark the banner line if the document has 3 or more SAP? &quot;MULTIPLE PROGRAMS&quot; after SAR. The phrase replaces the individual programme nicknames or [codewords]({{ 'codewords/' | relative_url }}) entirely. The change is deliberate rather than optional and reflects formal DoD marking guidance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideJuly 1, 2025 — When creating a banner line marking for two programs, add a singl...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why banners use “MULTIPLE PROGRAMS”

Replacing several programme names with **“MULTIPLE PROGRAMS”** serves several governance and security purposes.

First, it prevents the banner line from becoming excessively long. Banner markings appear prominently at the top and bottom of every page, on printed copies, and often in document management systems. A growing list of programme names can become difficult to read, difficult to reproduce consistently and more prone to formatting errors.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and procedures for the a...</span></span></span>

Second, the generic wording avoids exposing unnecessary programme identities to everyone who may encounter the document during handling. Even individuals already authorised for one of the programmes may not require knowledge of every other SAP represented in the document. Using a generic banner reduces the amount of programme-identifying information displayed on every page while still making it unmistakably clear that additional SAP restrictions apply.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and procedures for the a...</span></span></span>

Third, the convention standardises processing across security offices, records systems and automated marking tools. Software and document templates can recognise a single, predictable banner format rather than attempting to accommodate an unlimited number of programme combinations. DCSA's SAP templates and current SAP marking guidance are designed around these standardised formats.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dcsa.mil/Industrial-Security/Special-Access-Programs-Templates/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dcsa.mil">[DCSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dcsa.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs &amp; TemplatesThis web page has been established as a repository for SAP-specific processes, procedures, documen...</span></span></span>

## How the programme detail moves elsewhere

Using **“MULTIPLE PROGRAMS”** in the overall banner does **not** mean the individual SAPs disappear from the document's security markings.

Instead, the detailed programme identifiers move to more granular markings. For portions containing SAP information, DoD guidance requires the applicable programme identifiers (PIDs) to appear in the **[portion markings]({{ 'portion-markings/' | relative_url }})**, listed alphabetically and separated by slashes. Unlike the simplified banner, these portion markings preserve the precise programme associations needed for derivative classification, extraction and controlled reuse of individual paragraphs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideJuly 1, 2025 — When creating a banner line marking for two programs, add a singl...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2025</span></span></span>

This separation gives each marking level a distinct purpose:

* **Banner line:** alerts every handler that SAP controls apply and indicates either the specific programmes (when there are two) or that multiple SAPs are involved (when there are three or more).
* **Portion markings:** identify exactly which programme or programmes govern each classified section.
* **Programme records and security documentation:** maintain the complete accountability and access information needed by programme security officers and authorised repositories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/shorts/SAS0030-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Special Access Programs: Markings Short Student GuideJuly 1, 2025 — When creating a banner line marking for two programs, add a singl...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2025</span></span></span>

The result is a layered marking system in which the page header remains concise while the document still retains sufficient detail for proper handling throughout its lifecycle.

## Governance benefits of the simplified banner

The multiple-programme convention reflects an important principle in SAP governance: document markings are intended to communicate the information necessary for secure handling, not to function as a comprehensive inventory of every protected programme referenced.

Keeping the banner concise helps ensure that handlers recognise the presence of special-access controls immediately. It also reduces opportunities for inconsistent formatting across copies, scanned images and electronic document systems. Because [SAP markings]({{ 'markings/' | relative_url }}) must remain conspicuous and uniformly applied, predictable banner formats support compliance during creation, reproduction, storage and review.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/520507-V4p.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[WHS ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDoDM 5205.07, Volume 4, &quot;Special Access Program (SAP)...10 Oct 2013 — This Volume: (1) Provides guidance and procedures for the a...</span></span></span>

The approach also separates broad access warnings from detailed programme accountability. General handlers need an unmistakable signal that enhanced controls apply, whereas programme security personnel require the detailed identifiers maintained through portion markings, classification guidance and programme administration.
