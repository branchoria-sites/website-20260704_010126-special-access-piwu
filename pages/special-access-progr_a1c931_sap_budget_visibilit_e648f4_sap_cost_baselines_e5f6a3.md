## Introduction

Classified defence programmes do not become exempt from financial discipline simply because their capabilities are secret. On the contrary, the tighter the security surrounding a Special Access Program (SAP), the more important it is to maintain current, credible cost baselines and regularly review them against actual performance. Without an up-to-date baseline, decision-makers cannot distinguish between justified changes driven by evolving requirements and avoidable cost growth caused by technical problems, schedule slips or weak programme management.

This need is recognised in both law and defence [acquisition]({{ 'acquisition/' | relative_url }}) practice. Although much of a SAP's operational detail may remain classified, Congress is still entitled to receive information on programme [milestones]({{ 'milestones/' | relative_url }}), actual costs and current estimates. Those figures provide the reference point that allows oversight to occur without revealing sensitive capabilities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&amp;num=0&amp;req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title10-section119" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uscode.house.gov">[U.S. Code]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uscode.house.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Code10 USC 119: Special access programs: congressional...(iv) the estimated total cost of the program and the estimated cost of the prog...</span></span></span>

## What a baseline does in acquisition oversight

A cost baseline is more than a budget estimate. It establishes the approved expectations for a programme's cost, schedule and major performance objectives at a particular stage of acquisition. Future reviews compare actual progress against this agreed reference rather than against changing assumptions.

For a classified programme, this comparison performs several essential functions:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* It shows whether spending remains consistent with approved objectives.
* It highlights whether delays are beginning to increase programme costs.
* It allows oversight officials to distinguish between authorised scope changes and management failures.
* It supports informed decisions about whether additional funding is justified.

</div>

Without a baseline, almost any increase can be presented as an inevitable consequence of technical complexity or changing national security needs. A current baseline forces programme managers to explain why costs have changed and whether those changes represent better capability, unforeseen engineering challenges or simple execution problems.

US law reflects this principle. Under 10 U.S.C. §119, the Department of Defense provides the congressional defence committees with programme descriptions, major milestones, past actual costs and estimated costs for current and future fiscal years for SAPs, even though many programme details remain classified. The purpose is to preserve meaningful oversight despite operational secrecy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&amp;num=0&amp;req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title10-section119" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uscode.house.gov">[U.S. Code]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uscode.house.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Code10 USC 119: Special access programs: congressional...(iv) the estimated total cost of the program and the estimated cost of the prog...</span></span></span>

## How missing estimates weaken budget decisions

A classified programme competes for funding against every other defence priority. If its cost estimate is outdated, senior leaders are making trade-offs using obsolete information.

This creates several practical risks.

**Resources may be misallocated.** If actual programme costs have risen substantially but official estimates have not been updated, future [budgets]({{ 'budgets/' | relative_url }}) may underestimate the funding required, creating pressure for emergency reallocations or reductions elsewhere.

**Affordability becomes difficult to judge.** Decision-makers cannot assess whether additional investment still provides acceptable military value if the true programme cost is unknown or based on assumptions made years earlier.

**Cost growth becomes harder to detect.** Incremental increases can appear individually reasonable while collectively producing significant overruns that would have been obvious against a maintained baseline.

**Independent review becomes less effective.** Cost analysts, acquisition executives and congressional staff rely on current baseline data to identify emerging risks before they become programme crises.

The objective is not to eliminate [uncertainty]({{ 'uncertainty/' | relative_url }}). Highly advanced technologies often experience legitimate cost changes during development. Rather, the baseline provides a disciplined method for explaining and documenting those changes instead of allowing them to disappear behind classification.

## Why classified execution reviews matter

Routine execution reviews are especially important for SAPs because many of the normal external pressures that encourage [accountability]({{ 'accountability/' | relative_url }}) are absent.

Publicly acknowledged programmes often receive scrutiny from journalists, outside analysts, inspectors general and academic researchers. Unacknowledged or waived SAPs may instead be reviewed only within highly restricted government channels.

That makes internal acquisition reviews significantly more important. These reviews examine questions such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Are technical milestones still being achieved?
* Are contractor performance trends consistent with the approved plan?
* Have schedule delays increased programme cost?
* Do revised intelligence requirements justify additional expenditure?
* Should the acquisition strategy or programme structure be changed?

</div>

Because reviewers have access to classified technical information, they can assess whether rising costs correspond to genuine capability improvements rather than accepting higher expenditure without evidence.

Regular execution reviews therefore complement secrecy rather than conflict with it. Sensitive operational information remains protected while financial and programme performance continue to receive disciplined examination.

## Why rebaselining should not become an escape mechanism

Updating a cost baseline is sometimes necessary. Programmes can experience legitimate requirement changes, incorporate new technologies or respond to evolving operational threats. In such cases, an approved revised baseline provides a more realistic basis for future management.

However, repeatedly replacing baselines simply to accommodate overruns weakens accountability.

This concern has influenced broader defence acquisition policy. Congressional reforms to the Nunn–McCurdy framework introduced comparisons against both current and original baseline estimates, reducing the ability to mask persistent cost growth through repeated rebaselining alone. The Government Accountability Office has likewise noted that the original baseline remains the reference until an authorised revised acquisition programme baseline is approved. These reforms were intended to preserve visibility into long-term programme performance rather than allowing successive estimates to erase evidence of earlier overruns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/a97335.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[gao.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GAO-11-295R, Trends in Nunn-McCurdy Cost Breaches for...The original baseline estimate serves as the current baseline estimate until...</span></span></span>

Although not every SAP falls under the same reporting regime as major defence acquisition programmes, the underlying management principle is directly relevant: historical baselines provide essential context for judging whether programmes are genuinely improving or merely redefining success.

## Secrecy does not reduce the need for cost control

The central challenge in SAP oversight is separating operational secrecy from financial accountability. Protecting sensitive technologies may require limiting who knows a programme's purpose, location or technical characteristics, but it does not eliminate the need for reliable cost information.

Current baselines, milestone tracking and classified execution reviews allow authorised officials to answer fundamental questions without exposing sensitive capabilities:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Is the programme delivering what was approved?
* Are costs increasing for understandable reasons?
* Are schedule delays becoming financially significant?
* Does continued investment still represent good value relative to competing defence priorities?

</div>

In that sense, a current cost baseline is not merely an [accounting]({{ 'accounting/' | relative_url }}) document. It is the principal management tool that allows secret programmes to remain accountable within the classified oversight system. Without it, secrecy risks concealing ordinary acquisition drift as well as genuinely sensitive capabilities, making informed budget decisions substantially more difficult.

    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
