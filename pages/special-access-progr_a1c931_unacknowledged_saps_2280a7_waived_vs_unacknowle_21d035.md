## Introduction

Within the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) system of Special Access Programs (SAPs), the terms **unacknowledged** and **waived** are often treated as if they mean the same thing. They do not. An [unacknowledged SAP]({{ 'unacknowledged-7fdfef/' | relative_url }}) is one whose existence is itself classified. A waived SAP is a narrower category: it is an **unacknowledged SAP for which the Secretary of Defense has approved reduced congressional reporting under statutory authority because normal reporting would itself pose an unacceptable national security risk**. In other words, every waived SAP is unacknowledged, but not every unacknowledged SAP is waived.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...February 4, 2020 — Waived SAPs are unacknowledged SAPs for which the Secretary...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2020</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because public discussions sometimes assume that a "waived" programme operates outside oversight or beyond the law. The governing statutes and DoD policy describe something different. Waiver changes **how oversight is conducted**, not whether oversight exists. The result is a more restrictive reporting regime, not the elimination of legal [accountability]({{ 'accountability/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/520511p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoD Instruction 5205.11, &quot;Management, Administration...12 Sept 2024 — The Secretary of Defense may designate select unacknowledged S...</span></span></span>

## What waived status changes

An unacknowledged SAP already protects the programme's existence, purpose and technical details from anyone without authorised access. Waived status adds another layer by modifying the normal congressional notification process.

Under current DoD policy, the Secretary of Defense may designate **selected [unacknowledged SAPs]({{ 'unacknowledged/' | relative_url }})** as waived when following the ordinary statutory reporting procedures would itself adversely affect national security. Even then, the programme remains subject to congressional oversight, although information is provided to a much smaller group of designated congressional leaders.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/520511p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoD Instruction 5205.11, &quot;Management, Administration...12 Sept 2024 — The Secretary of Defense may designate select unacknowledged S...</span></span></span>

In practical terms, waived [status changes]({{ 'status-changes/' | relative_url }}) several things:

* **Congressional reporting becomes more limited.** Instead of broader notification requirements, access is generally restricted to specified committee leadership and selected authorised personnel within the defence oversight structure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/520511p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoD Instruction 5205.11, &quot;Management, Administration...12 Sept 2024 — The Secretary of Defense may designate select unacknowledged S...</span></span></span>
* **Access controls become more restrictive.** DoD training materials explicitly identify waived SAPs as having tighter reporting and access controls than other unacknowledged SAPs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...February 4, 2020 — Waived SAPs are unacknowledged SAPs for which the Secretary...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2020</span></span></span>
* **[Approval authority]({{ 'approval-authority/' | relative_url }}) is concentrated.** Only the Secretary or Deputy Secretary of Defense may approve key decisions affecting waived programmes, reflecting their exceptional sensitivity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/520511p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoD Instruction 5205.11, &quot;Management, Administration...12 Sept 2024 — The Secretary of Defense may designate select unacknowledged S...</span></span></span>

The rationale is that even the process of routine congressional notification can reveal operational information if a programme is exceptionally sensitive—for example, by exposing a capability under development, a particularly fragile [intelligence]({{ 'intelligence/' | relative_url }}) source or a highly classified military technology.

## What unacknowledged status changes

Unacknowledged status addresses a different question: **whether the government publicly admits that the programme exists at all**.

An acknowledged SAP may be discussed in general terms even though its operational details remain classified. By contrast, an unacknowledged SAP protects both the existence and purpose of the programme from unauthorised disclosure. Funding, organisational relationships and programme names may also receive additional protection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...February 4, 2020 — Waived SAPs are unacknowledged SAPs for which the Secretary...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2020</span></span></span>

Importantly, unacknowledged status does **not** automatically change congressional oversight. Under the standard statutory framework, unacknowledged SAPs are still reported to [Congress]({{ 'congress/' | relative_url }}) through established oversight procedures. The difference lies in public visibility rather than in the existence of oversight itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://irp.fas.org/doddir/dod/5200-1r/chapter_8.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: irp.fas.org">[Intelligence Resource Program]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">irp.fas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Intelligence Resource ProgramChapter 8 SPECIAL ACCESS PROGRAMSBefore initiating a SAP, notifying Congress, or expending funds on a SAP, t...</span></span></span>

This distinction is frequently overlooked in public debate. A programme can be entirely hidden from public acknowledgement while still existing within established legal reporting structures.

## Where the two categories overlap

The relationship between the two classifications is hierarchical rather than parallel.

CharacteristicUnacknowledged SAPWaived SAPProgramme existence publicly acknowledgedNoNoProgramme details classifiedYesYesProgramme is a type of unacknowledged SAPNo (general category)YesCongressional reporting modified by statutory waiverNoYesAdditional reporting and access restrictionsNoYes

The key point is that **waived status builds on unacknowledged status rather than replacing it**. A waived SAP first satisfies the criteria for being unacknowledged and then receives additional protection because standard reporting procedures are judged to present an unacceptable security risk.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...February 4, 2020 — Waived SAPs are unacknowledged SAPs for which the Secretary...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why the distinction is often misunderstood

Several factors contribute to confusion.

First, the word **"waived"** naturally suggests that rules have been suspended altogether. In reality, the statutory waiver concerns particular congressional reporting procedures rather than the broader legal framework governing SAPs. Oversight continues, although through a narrower channel.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/520511p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoD Instruction 5205.11, &quot;Management, Administration...12 Sept 2024 — The Secretary of Defense may designate select unacknowledged S...</span></span></span>

Second, discussions of highly classified programmes often blur different kinds of secrecy. Public commentary may use terms such as "black programme", "unacknowledged", "waived", "deep black" or similar expressions interchangeably even though official DoD guidance assigns them distinct meanings. Training materials consistently distinguish between acknowledged, unacknowledged and waived protection levels.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[CDSE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...February 4, 2020 — Waived SAPs are unacknowledged SAPs for which the Secretary...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2020</span></span></span>

Third, many historical references to waived SAPs emerged from reporting on exceptionally secret defence programmes, encouraging the impression that waiver itself denotes extraordinary or unlawful activity. ## Why the distinction matters for oversight

Confusing waived SAPs with unacknowledged SAPs can distort debates about transparency and accountability.

If an analyst assumes every hidden programme is waived, they may overstate how restricted congressional access actually is. Conversely, assuming that waived programmes receive no oversight ignores the statutory framework under which selected congressional leaders continue to receive notifications and briefings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/520511p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: esd.whs.mil">[ESD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">esd.whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DoD Instruction 5205.11, &quot;Management, Administration...12 Sept 2024 — The Secretary of Defense may designate select unacknowledged S...</span></span></span>

The practical policy trade-off is therefore not **oversight versus no oversight**, but **broader oversight versus more tightly concentrated oversight**. Waived status represents an exceptional adjustment to reporting procedures for a small subset of the most sensitive unacknowledged SAPs, whereas unacknowledged status primarily concerns protecting the programme's existence from public disclosure. Keeping those two concepts separate is essential for understanding how the U.S. SAP system balances operational secrecy with constitutional oversight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/student-guides/SA001-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdse.edu">[cdse.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdse.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Student Guide Course: Special Access Program (SAP)...February 4, 2020 — Waived SAPs are unacknowledged SAPs for which the Secretary...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2020</span></span></span>
